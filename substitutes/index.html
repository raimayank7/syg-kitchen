Before you swap

  • A substitute gets you through the recipe. It rarely tastes exactly the same, so try it first in everyday cooking, not on the day guests come.
  • In baking, change one ingredient at a time. Two swaps in one cake make it hard to know what went wrong.
  • Sour swaps such as lemon juice go in at the end of cooking, off the heat, so they do not turn bitter.

Questions people ask

What can I use instead of eggs in a cake?

For each egg, a quarter cup of thick curd or mashed ripe banana works in most simple cakes and muffins. They bind the batter, so keep the baking powder or soda in the recipe for the rise.

Can I use baking soda instead of baking powder?

Only with something sour. A quarter teaspoon of baking soda with half a cup of curd or buttermilk does the work of one teaspoon of baking powder.

Is cornflour the same as makki ka atta?

No. Cornflour in Indian shops is the fine white starch used for thickening. Makki ka atta is yellow maize flour for rotis. One cannot replace the other.
