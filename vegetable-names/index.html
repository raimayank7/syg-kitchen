About the seasons

The season shown is when the vegetable is most plentiful and cheapest in the plains of north India. Cold stores and transport now bring most vegetables all year, and seasons shift in the south, the hills and the coast. In season, a vegetable usually tastes better and costs less.

The gourd family, sorted out

  • Lauki (bottle gourd): long or round, pale green and smooth.
  • Tori (ridge gourd): long, dark green, with raised ridges along its length.
  • Tinda (apple gourd): small, round and light green, the size of a small apple.
  • Parwal (pointed gourd): short and plump with pointed ends and pale stripes.
  • Kundru (ivy gourd): finger-sized, striped, looks like a tiny cucumber.
  • Karela (bitter gourd): bumpy skin and bitter taste.

Questions people ask

What is tori called in English?

Ridge gourd. The smooth kind without ridges is called sponge gourd.

What is tinda in English?

Apple gourd, also called Indian round gourd.

What is arbi in English?

Colocasia or taro root.

What is the difference between jimikand and arbi?

Jimikand (suran) is elephant foot yam, a large rough corm. Arbi (colocasia) is small and hairy. Both are cooked well before eating.
