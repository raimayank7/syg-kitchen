How long food keeps in the fridge and freezer

A quick guide to storage times for leftovers, meat, fish, eggs and everyday Indian kitchen items, with the simple rules that keep food safe.

Check with the official source. Rows marked "food safety guidance" follow the cold storage guidance of the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service. Five rules that matter more than the table

  1. Two hours. Cooked food should not sit at room temperature for more than two hours, or one hour in very hot weather.
  2. Cool fast. Spread hot food in shallow containers so it cools quickly, then refrigerate. A deep pot of hot dal stays warm in the middle for hours.
  3. Cover and separate. Keep raw meat, fish and eggs covered and below cooked food, so nothing drips onto food that will not be cooked again.
  4. Reheat until steaming. Bring curries and dals to a boil and heat other food until it is hot all the way through.
  5. Freezing pauses, it does not clean. Freezer times are about taste and texture. Food kept frozen stays safe, but germs wake up again when it thaws, so thaw in the fridge, not on the counter.

Signs that food should be thrown away

  • A sour or unusual smell, bubbles or fizz in a dal or curry, or a slimy feel.
  • Any mould on soft food such as bread, paneer, cooked food or soft fruit. Cutting the spot off is not enough.
  • A swollen packet or tin.

Food can also be unsafe without any sign at all, which is why the time limits matter.

Questions people ask

How long can cooked food stay out?

Not more than two hours at room temperature, and not more than one hour in hot weather above about 32 °C. After that, refrigerate it or throw it away.

Can I reheat food twice?

It is safer to reheat only the portion you will eat, once, until it is steaming hot all through. Each round of cooling and reheating gives germs another chance to grow.

What temperature should the fridge be?

4 °C or colder for the fridge and minus 18 °C for the freezer. Do not pack the fridge so full that air cannot move.

Is it safe to eat rice the next day?

Yes, if it was cooled quickly and kept in the fridge within about an hour of cooking. Reheat it until steaming hot and do not keep it for long.
