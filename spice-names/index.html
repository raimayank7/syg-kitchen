Names that are often mixed up

  • Jeera and shah jeera: cumin is the everyday brown seed. Shah jeera (caraway, also sold as black cumin) is darker, thinner and sweeter, and is used in biryani.
  • Kalonji is not onion seed: the small black seeds on naan are nigella. They are sometimes sold as "onion seeds" or "black cumin", but come from a different plant.
  • Tej patta and bay leaf: the Indian tej patta is the leaf of a cinnamon relative and has three long veins. The European bay leaf has one central vein and a different smell.
  • Saunf and ajwain: fennel is green, sweet and larger. Ajwain is tiny, grey-brown and sharp.
  • Badi and chhoti elaichi: green cardamom is for sweets and tea. Black cardamom is smoky and only for savoury dishes.

Keeping spices fresh

  • Buy whole spices and grind small amounts. Ground spices lose their smell within a few months.
  • Store in airtight jars away from the stove and sunlight.
  • Roast whole spices on low heat for a minute before grinding to bring the aroma out.

Questions people ask

What is ajwain called in English?

Carom seeds. You may also see the names bishop's weed or ajowan.

What is kalonji in English?

Nigella seeds.

What is the Hindi name of asafoetida?

Hing. It is sold as a powder mixed with flour, or as a stronger solid lump.

What is javitri?

Mace, the red lacy covering around the nutmeg (jaiphal) seed.
