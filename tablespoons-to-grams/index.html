Tablespoons and teaspoons to grams

A spoon measures space, and grams measure weight, so there is no single answer to "how many grams is a tablespoon". A tablespoon of honey weighs about three times as much as a tablespoon of flour. This chart gives the weight for each common ingredient, for a level 15 ml tablespoon and a level 5 ml teaspoon.

Level, rounded and heaped

Unless a recipe says otherwise, a spoon measure is level: fill the spoon, then sweep a knife or a finger across the top. The chart is for level spoons.

  • A rounded spoon has a gentle dome and holds about one and a half times a level spoon.
  • A heaped spoon is piled as high as it will go and can hold double, or more with flour and powdered sugar.
  • A scant spoon is a little less than level.

The gap between a level and a heaped spoon is the most common reason a cake turns out dry or a dal turns out salty. For baking powder, baking soda and salt, always measure level.

Measuring spoons and eating spoons

The teaspoon and tablespoon in a recipe are measuring spoons of 5 ml and 15 ml. Spoons from the cutlery drawer are not made to a standard. A steel dinner spoon often holds only 7 to 10 ml, and a small tea spoon may hold 2.5 to 4 ml. If you have no measuring spoons, check your own spoons once: an empty 5 ml or 10 ml medicine cap or measuring cup from a syrup bottle is an accurate measure that most homes already have.

Quick answers from the chart

  • Sugar: a tablespoon is about 12.5 g and a teaspoon about 4 g.
  • Salt: a teaspoon is about 6 g.
  • Ghee and butter: a tablespoon is about 13 g of ghee or 14 g of butter.
  • Oil: a tablespoon is about 14 g.
  • Atta and maida: a tablespoon is about 8 g.
  • Honey: a tablespoon is about 21 g.

Going the other way: grams to spoons

Divide the grams you need by the weight of one spoon. A recipe asks for 50 g of sugar: 50 divided by 12.5 is 4 tablespoons. It asks for 20 g of butter: about one and a half tablespoons. For amounts above about 60 g, it is easier and more accurate to use a cup measure or a scale.

Why sticky and powdery ingredients mislead

  • Honey, jaggery syrup and ghee cling to the spoon, so less reaches the bowl than you measured. Oil the spoon lightly first and they slide off.
  • Flour and cocoa pack down. Stir the flour in its container, spoon it up lightly and level it. Do not press the spoon against the side of the tin.
  • Coarse and fine salt differ. The chart is for fine table salt. Rock salt and coarse sea salt have more air between the grains, so a spoon of them weighs less.

When a spoon is not good enough

For everyday cooking, spoons are fine. For bread, cakes and sweets where sugar must reach a stage, weigh the main ingredients. A small digital kitchen scale costs little and removes the guesswork. Without one, see measuring without a scale.

Questions people ask

How many grams is one tablespoon of sugar?

About 12.5 grams for granulated sugar, measured level in a 15 ml tablespoon.

How many grams is one teaspoon of salt?

About 6 grams of fine table salt. Coarse salt weighs less per spoon.

How many grams is a tablespoon of butter or ghee?

About 14 grams of butter and about 13 grams of ghee.

Is a tablespoon the same as a big steel spoon?

Not reliably. A measuring tablespoon is 15 ml. Dinner spoons vary and often hold only 7 to 10 ml.
