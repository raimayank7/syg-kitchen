Ten kitchen facts worth remembering

  1. 1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoons = 15 ml.
  2. 1 cup = 16 tablespoons = 240 ml.
  3. 1 cup of atta is about 120 g; 1 cup of sugar is about 200 g.
  4. 1 cup of raw rice is about 185 g and feeds two to three people.
  5. 180 °C = 350 °F = gas mark 4, the usual heat for cakes.
  6. In a fan oven, set about 20 °C lower.
  7. 1 ounce is about 28 g; 1 pound is about 454 g.
  8. "Ek pav" in the market is 250 g.
  9. Soak rajma and chana for 8 hours before cooking.
  10. Cooked food should not stay out for more than two hours.

Questions people ask

Is this site free?

Yes. There is no login and nothing to install. The converters work inside your browser.

Which cup size do you use?

A 240 ml cup, the size on most measuring cup sets and food labels. The measures page explains the 250 ml metric cup as well.

Are the weights exact?

They are close guides. A cup of flour changes with how it is filled. For baking, weigh in grams when you can.
